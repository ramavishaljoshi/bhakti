# Naam Jaap Counter — Brand Analysis, Logo Options & Name Ideas

## 1. What the website actually is

A **free, private digital mala (108-bead) counter** for chanting practice. Core promises:

- **108-count japa** — the sacred mala round at the centre of the product.
- **Local-first / private** — every completed mala is saved in the browser; no signup, no forced account. Optional Google Sheets backup only if you want it.
- **Multi-mantra** — Radhe Radhe, Hare Krishna, Ram Naam, Om Namah Shivaya, Gayatri, Waheguru, or a custom mantra.
- **A small family of tools** — Naam Jaap Counter, Kids Counter, Karma Reset, Daily Affirmations, Sadhana Tracker, Mantra Timer, Gayatri Counter.

**Canonical domain:** `naamjapa.com` (the Netlify URL is a staging mirror).

## 3. Better / unique name ideas

"Naam Jaap Counter" is descriptive and good for SEO, but generic and hard to own as a brand. Below are stronger, more brandable options grouped by direction. (Check live `.com` availability before committing.)

### A. Devotional & authentic (recommended direction)

- **JapaMala** ⭐ — *japa* (repetition) + *mala* (the bead loop). Clear, devotional, instantly understood, and pairs perfectly with logo #1/#5. Strongest all-rounder.
- **Sumiran** (also *Sumeran/Smaran*) — "loving remembrance of the divine name." Calm, soft, distinctive, not generic.
- **Ajapa** — from *ajapa-japa*, "effortless, spontaneous chanting." Short, modern-sounding, spiritually deep, easy to brand.
- **NaamMala** — a gentle bridge from your current name into a bead-led identity; keeps "Naam" for continuity.

### B. Modern app feel

- **MalaTap** — names the exact interaction (tap the bead). Memorable, friendly, very "app store."
- **BeadCount** / **Beadful** — light, approachable, English-first.
- **JapFlow** — emphasises the calm, unbroken rhythm of practice.

### C. Number-led (leans on the sacred 108)

- **Mala108** / **One‑Oh‑Eight** — owns the number every practitioner recognises.
- **108 Beads** — simple, descriptive, brandable.

### D. Keep-SEO, add-brand (have both)

Keep "Naam Jaap Counter" as the descriptive product/page title for search, but adopt a short brand name on top of it — e.g. **"JapaMala — Naam Jaap Counter."** You get discoverability *and* a name people can remember and say.

---

### Top 3 to shortlist

1. **JapaMala** — best balance of meaning, clarity, and brandability.
2. **Ajapa** — most distinctive and modern while staying authentic.
3. **Sumiran** — softest, most emotional, great if the affirmations/wellbeing side grows.

## 4. Research-backed direction (manka · manka pher · jaap)

You also have **emotional / mind tools** (Karma Reset, Daily Affirmations, Mantra Timer, Sadhana Tracker), not just a chanting counter. So the umbrella brand needs to cover *both* japa **and** inner/emotional work. The research below points to one concept that does exactly that.

### What the words mean

- **Manka** (मनका) — a single **bead** of the mala. (Punjabi *manka* = bead; *manke* = beads.)
- **Mala** — the full string of 108 beads (the garland).
- **Jaap / Japa** — the act of repeating the divine name; Sikhs call it *Naam japna*.
- **Manka pher / phera** — to **turn the bead** (one rotation = one repetition).

### The key insight — a perfect pun for your product

There is a famous **Kabir doha** that is almost a tagline written 600 years ago:

> *"Mala pherat jug bhaya, phira na **man ka** pher.*
> *Kar ka **manka** chhod de, **man ka manka** pher."*
>
> "Ages passed turning the rosary, yet the mind never turned.
> Drop the bead in your **hand** — turn the bead of the **mind**."

The pun is the gold: **manka** = "a bead" *and* **man ka** = "of the mind" (identical pronunciation). Kabir's whole point is that real practice isn't mechanically counting beads in the hand — it's **turning the bead of the mind**.

That is *literally your product line*: the **Naam Jaap counter** is the bead in the hand; **Karma Reset / Affirmations / Mantra Timer** are the bead of the mind. One word ties the chanting tools and the emotional tools together.

### Recommended brand: **Manka**

- **Meaning:** "the bead" — and, by the pun, "of the mind." Covers japa tools *and* emotional/mind tools under one roof.
- **Tagline:** **"Man ka manka pher"** — *turn the bead of the mind.* (Rooted in Kabir, instantly resonant to an Indian audience.)
- **Pronunceable, short, soft, ownable** as a spiritual brand.
- **Heads-up on the namespace:** "Manka" exists in app stores for unrelated things (a food-ordering app, a gifts app, a design studio) — **none in meditation/spiritual**, so the category is open, but for a clean handle/domain prefer a distinct spelling or lockup:
  - **Mankaa** (double-a) — better domain/handle availability.
  - **Manka Jaap** — pairs the bead with the practice; very clear.
  - **Manka Pher** / **Phera** — "the turning of the bead"; *Phera* alone is soft and brandable.
  - **Man Ka** (two words) — leans into the "of the mind" pun for the emotional side.

### How it maps to your tools

| Tool | Bead it turns |
|---|---|
| Naam Jaap Counter, Gayatri Counter | *kar ka manka* — the bead in the hand |
| Karma Reset, Daily Affirmations, Mantra Timer | *man ka manka* — the bead of the mind |

### Updated top shortlist (umbrella brand)

1. **Manka** ⭐ (or **Mankaa** for a cleaner handle) — "turn the bead of the mind." Covers chanting + emotional tools; deep, brandable, Kabir-rooted.
2. **JapaMala** — best if you stay focused on the counter alone.
3. **Phera / Manka Pher** — "the turning"; calm and distinctive.

> Keep **"Naam Jaap Counter"** as the descriptive product/page title for SEO, and put **Manka** on top as the umbrella brand — e.g. *"Manka · Naam Jaap Counter."* You get search traffic and a memorable name.
